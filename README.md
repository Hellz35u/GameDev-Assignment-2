# ⚔️ Stand Steel

## 🎮 About the Game

**Stand Steel** is a 2D survival game where your main goal is to **survive all enemy waves and achieve the highest score possible!**

The player must fight through multiple waves of enemies and survive all the way to the **final wave**.

Throughout the battlefield, players can also collect **Collectables** that increase their score. This means that surviving is not your only challenge — try to collect as many Collectables as possible and finish the game with the **highest score you can achieve**.

If things get too difficult, you are not completely alone. During the game, you can open the **Pause Menu** and request help. An **Ally** will spawn on the battlefield and fight alongside you, helping you defeat enemies and survive the upcoming waves.

Can you survive every wave, collect as many points as possible, and conquer **Stand Steel**?

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move Right | `Right Arrow →` |
| Move Left | `Left Arrow ←` |
| Jump | `Space` |
| Attack | `Left Ctrl` or `Left Mouse Button` |

---

## 💎 Collectables & Score

Collectables can be found throughout the game.

Picking up a Collectable will increase your **Score**.

While your primary objective is to survive until the final wave, you should also try to collect as many Collectables as possible.

Challenge yourself to complete the game with the **highest score possible!**

---

## 🛡️ Ally System

Need some help during the battle?

Open the **Pause Menu** and select **Ask for Help**.

An Ally will spawn into the game and assist you in combat by attacking enemies and helping you survive the waves.

---

## 🏆 Objectives

Your objectives are to:

- Survive all enemy waves.
- Defeat the enemies attacking you.
- Collect **Collectables** to increase your score.
- Try to achieve the **highest score possible**.
- Request help from an Ally when needed.
- Reach and complete the **final wave**.

Survive the final wave and finish with the best score you can to successfully complete **Stand Steel**.
